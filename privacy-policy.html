Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 July 2026

The short version: your subscriptions never leave your phone. There is no account, we never ask for your bank, and we cannot see what you track. The only thing we receive is anonymous usage and crash data that tells us the app works — never what you pay for.

This policy explains what CancelSubscriptions ("the app", "we") does with data. It applies to the iOS and Android apps. If anything here is unclear, email umairkhalidsidd@gmail.com and ask — a real person answers.

01What we collect

Your subscription data — stays on your device

Everything you enter — service names, prices, billing cycles, renewal dates, budgets, notes and your savings history — is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded, never synced to a server, and we have no way to read it. There is no account to create and no login.

Anonymous usage data

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand whether the app is actually being used — how many people open it, and where they get stuck. These events carry no subscription information whatsoever: no names, no amounts, no currencies, no dates.

Crash reports

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics. If the app crashes, it sends a technical report — the code path that failed, device model, OS version and app version — so the bug can be fixed. Crash reports do not contain your subscription data.

Notifications

Renewal and trial reminders are scheduled locally, by your phone. They are not push notifications: nothing is sent from a server, and no one is told when your subscriptions renew.

02What we never do

  • We never connect to your bank. The app has no bank linking, no Plaid, no open banking, no card access.
  • We never sell or rent your data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites for advertising.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, address or phone number.
  • We cannot see your subscriptions. Not because we promise not to look — because they never reach us.

03Third-party services

The app includes Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics), which processes the anonymous data described above. Their handling is governed by Firebase's privacy documentation and Google's Privacy Policy.

When you tap a cancel link, the app opens the service's own website (Netflix, Spotify and so on) in your browser. From that moment you are on their site, under their privacy policy — we are not involved in, and cannot see, what happens there.

04Cookies and tracking

The app is not a website and uses no cookies. Firebase Analytics uses a randomly generated app instance identifier, which is not linked to your identity and is reset if you delete the app. We use no advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking.

05How long data is kept

  • On your device: for as long as you keep the app. Deleting the app deletes all of it, immediately and permanently. We keep no copy, so we cannot restore it — export a backup first if you want one.
  • Analytics and crash data: retained by Google Firebase under its standard retention periods, then deleted automatically.

06Children's privacy

CancelSubscriptions is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Since the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing about a child we could hold. If you believe otherwise, contact us and we will investigate.

07Data sharing

We share nothing, because we hold nothing about you. The only data leaving your device is the anonymous analytics and crash information above, which goes to Google Firebase as our processor. We would disclose data if legally compelled — but in practice we have no personal data to hand over.

08Your rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA and similar laws you have rights to access, correct, export and delete your personal data. Because your subscription data lives only on your phone, you exercise most of these directly and instantly:

  • Access and export: You → Export my data writes a full JSON copy.
  • Correct: edit or delete any entry in the app.
  • Delete everything: delete the app. That erases all of it.
  • Opt out of analytics: use your OS controls — iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, or Android Settings → Google → Ads. You can also email us to request deletion of analytics data associated with your device.

09Security

Your data sits in the app's private storage, protected by your phone's operating system sandbox and, if enabled, its device encryption. No app can read another app's private storage. Because we run no server that holds your data, there is no database of subscriptions to breach.

10Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be announced in the app's release notes. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
